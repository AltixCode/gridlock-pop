Privacy Policy

BlockJam · Last updated 13 September 2026

BlockJam is a puzzle game published by AltixCode. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no server of ours. Your scores and settings stay on your device. This policy explains the limited data the app's two third-party services process.

What we collect ourselves

Nothing. Your high score, settings and progress are stored only in the app's local storage on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

What third parties process

ServiceDataWhy
Google AdMob Advertising identifier, device and general location (country-level), ad interactions To show ads and measure them. Ads fund the free version.
RevenueCat Store purchase receipt, an anonymous app user id, device type To confirm your "Remove Ads" purchase and restore it on your other devices.
Apple App Store / Google Play Payment details They process payment. We never see or store your card details.

Advertising and tracking

On iOS the app asks, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether you allow tracking. If you decline, ads still appear but are not personalised, and the game is otherwise identical. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising id in Settings → Privacy → Ads. Buying the one-time "Remove Ads" upgrade stops all advertising requests from the app.

Children

BlockJam is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. Ad content is restricted to Google's "G" rating.

Your choices

  • Decline tracking (iOS) or reset your advertising id (Android) at any time.
  • Buy the ad-free upgrade to stop all ad requests.
  • Uninstall the app to delete everything stored locally.
  • Ask us to delete the data held by our providers by emailing support@altixcode.com.
